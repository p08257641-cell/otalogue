Our Founder & Mission

"Healthcare Professionals Deserve Comfort, Dignity & Impeccable Style."

"As a student at Family Health University College, I saw colleagues struggling with uncomfortable, ill-fitting scrubs during intense clinical rotations. I created MedROBE to blend functional medical engineering with refined aesthetics."

Founded by Charlene Odei Asare, MedROBE and accessories by lene is dedicated to equipping medical, nursing, and allied health professionals with high-performance scrubs, tailored labcoats, and dependable clinical diagnostic instruments.

From our campus operations based at Room M306 at Family Health University College in Teshie, Accra, we have fitted hundreds of students for white coat ceremonies, OSCE exams, and ward rounds across Ghanaian hospitals.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ordering, sizing, embroidery, and campus pickup.

Where is the pickup hub on campus? +
You can pick up your order directly at Room M306, Family Health University College (FHUC) in Teshie, Accra. We also offer direct handoff to FHUC student hostels and dispatch across Accra and nationwide.
How do I complete my order? +
Simply add your desired scrubs, labcoats, stethoscopes, or kits to the online bag, and click "Send Order via WhatsApp". This generates a structured order message sent directly to Charlene at 0245636351 for instant confirmation.
Can I customize embroidery with my name and department? +
Yes! Our interactive Embroidery Studio lets you enter your exact title, name, and department (e.g. Dr. Charlene Odei Asare / Family Health Medical School) in gold, white, or navy thread with high precision needle stitching.
What diagnostic items are in the Student Medical Kit? +
The Complete Diagnostic Kit includes a calibrated aneroid blood pressure sphygmomanometer cuff, dual-head acoustic stethoscope, Taylor percussion reflex hammer, C128 neurological tuning fork, LED diagnostic pupil penlight, trauma shears, and padded zippered carry organizer.
What payment methods are accepted? +
We accept MTN Mobile Money (MoMo), Telecel Cash, Bank Transfer, and Cash on Campus Pickup at Room M306.
